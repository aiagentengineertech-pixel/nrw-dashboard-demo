# NRW Decision Dashboard: worked example

NRW Decision Dashboard v0.4: what a small utility could see with low-cost monitoring and a model, built on open data. Four views: leak alarms on the L-Town 2018 benchmark year (results from nrw-detection), zone losses from the fully metered Area C balance, drought supply schedules chosen by full-data and data-lean models (L-Town, Christchurch, Kitchener), and a supply-hours what-if on the Christchurch Mt Pleasant 3 network.

Leslie Allan Marshall, Allan Engineering Software Development (ORCID 0009-0009-9218-5674).

Data: L-Town, BattLeDIM 2020 (Vrachimis et al., CC BY 4.0); Christchurch City Council Water Supply Open Data (CC BY 4.0); City of Kitchener (Open Government Licence). Research reconstruction; not for detailed design.
