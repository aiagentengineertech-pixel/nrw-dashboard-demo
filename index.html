NRW Decision Dashboard

Hydraulic decision tools for small water utilities, built on EPANET 2.2 and WNTR. An engineer asks a question in plain language and gets a map, a table and a recommendation they can check. The example below is a real run on an open-data network: Christchurch's Mt Pleasant 3 zone (497 junctions, 16 km of main) under a four-hour daily supply.

Low-pressure areas and likely causes (4-hour supply)

The tool groups failing junctions into areas and separates the two problems a utility fixes differently: not enough static head (needs a booster or a new zone) and too much friction loss (needs bigger or parallel pipes).

AreaJunctionsLikely causeSuggested action
149Pipe capacityAbout 38.9 m lost to friction on the route from the source. Upsize or parallel the 100 mm mains listed (P95, P251, P236, P250, P91).
27High groundStatic head is only −0.9 m at the highest point. Pipe upgrades will not fix this: booster pump, higher tank or separate pressure zone.
37High ground and pipe capacityStatic head 13.2 m and 37.3 m friction loss. Upsizing helps; a booster or separate zone may still be needed at peak.
