Area C: losses from the zone balance against the true leak flow

Estimated from the balance: inlet minus metered use minus tank change (L/s)True leak flow (L/s)

Area C is the one part of L-Town with an inlet meter, a tank level and a meter on every customer. Closing the balance each month reads the zone's leakage directly, including a 1.9 L/s leak that runs all year below every network-wide alarm. Hourly the balance is too noisy (tank-level steps); monthly it is exact to 0.1 L/s. The January offset (0.49 L/s) is removed, so it measures change from January.

    • supply_schedule · compare_schedulesIntermittent supply with pressure-dependent demand
    • optimise_rationingShares limited source water across zones and hours
    • low_pressure_reportWho is under-supplied, where, and why
    • pressure_managementSettings that hold a minimum service pressure
    • pipe_upgrade_whatifEffect of upsizing selected mains
    • recommend_loggersWhere to put a limited number of loggers

    • leak_location_rankRanks likely leak locations from pressure data
    • export_qgis_layers · log_decisionQGIS layers and a record of each decision

    How this page works: every scenario shown here was run in advance with the dashboard (EPANET 2.2 through WNTR, pressure-dependent demand, supply starting 05:00, 10 m service threshold) and stored in the page, so it opens instantly on any device. In use, an engineer runs the same tools on their own network by asking in plain language.
